If things slip

Two decision points — leaving the house, and leaving the lighthouse

Before you go: the whole ride is OPTIONAL — it's a rest day. Skipping it entirely just means the Kilrush brim moves to tomorrow's first miles (the Day 8 route passes the same forecourt).

15:10, walking back to the bikes at the lighthouse: if the weather is closing or anyone's done, skip Bridges of Ross and the West End coffee — R487 straight through Cross to Kilkee, brim at Kilrush 16:15, home ≈16:45.

The one keep: if you ride at all, keep the Kilrush brim — 69 mi are gone since Miltown Malbay and tomorrow is the long day east.
