If things slip

One decision point — 14:00, walking back to the bikes at the Cliffs

If the day is on time (away by 14:00): ride on north to Doolin for lunch at Fitz's, then the Burren fringe and the N67 home. Querrin ≈16:55.

If you are projecting past 17:00: turn SOUTH on the R478 instead — 3 miles to Vaughan's Anchor Inn, Liscannor (open daily 12:00–20:30, 065 708 1548, free parking, Michelin-listed seafood), then Lahinch and straight down the N67. Saves 11 miles and 25 minutes.

The one thing to keep either way: the Miltown Malbay brim. Wednesday is Loop Head and there is nothing verified to fill at near the house.
